\noindent\textbf{Funding:} This research received no external funding.

\noindent\textbf{Data Availability Statement:} All artifacts, configuration files, gate
definitions and the figure script are in the project repository. Figures regenerate with
\texttt{python papers/p2\_plane/make\_figures.py} and all numerical results with
\texttt{make reproduce}. The library is available at
\url{https://pypi.org/project/strataq/} (accessed on 12 August 2026). Road-network data are
from the Transportation Networks repository (Sioux Falls); electricity data are CAISO
day-ahead locational marginal prices at node TH\_SP15\_GEN-APND; retail scanner data are
derived from the Dominick's Finer Foods database (Kilts Center) and redistribution of
derived artifacts is subject to CC-BY-NC-4.0.
