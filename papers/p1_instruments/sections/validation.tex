Four of the calibration rows deserve their own paragraph, because in them an
external implementation or a theorem someone else proved --- not our own algebra
--- decides whether we are right.

\paragraph{Against \texttt{pygambit}, strategic form.} \texttt{pygambit}
\citep{gambit} implements the logit tracing homotopy of \citet{turocy2005}. On 32
seeded games at two precisions each, our fixed-$\lambda$ profiles agree with it
to a maximum profile gap of $1.2\times10^{-9}$ against a declared oracle
tolerance of $10^{-8}$ (\texttt{gambit\_agreement}). The branch tracer is checked
the same way but along the correspondence rather than at a point, reproducing the
direct solve at fixed-$\lambda$ crossings to $8.6\times10^{-13}$
(\texttt{branch\_agreement}). \texttt{pygambit} is an optional test-time oracle,
never a runtime requirement.

\paragraph{Against \texttt{pygambit}, extensive form.} The agent QRE is
cross-validated against \texttt{pygambit}'s extensive-form logit solver on four
classic trees --- entry deterrence at $\lambda\in\{0.5,2.0,5.0\}$, the four-stage
centipede, Selten's horse and Kuhn poker --- each required to sit inside the
$10^{-8}$ oracle tolerance in maximum behaviour-strategy gap. This check lives in
the test suite rather than in a committed artifact, and it is \texttt{skipif}-ed
when \texttt{pygambit} is absent, so a CI run without the optional dependency
passes a suite whose oracle checks never executed. That is a real gap in the
evidence and it is named again in Section~8.

\paragraph{Against finite differences, and against a second derivative path.}
That $\chieq=(I-SB)^{-1}S$ is the equilibrium's derivative is checked two
independent ways. Central finite differences of the solved equilibrium under a
payoff perturbation agree with the closed form to $1.3\times10^{-8}$ across 50
random games at a declared finite-difference tolerance of $10^{-6}$
(\texttt{chi\_fd\_agreement}); the residual is truncation, not solver error.
Independently, the implicit-differentiation VJP --- which solves the transposed
tangent system instead of forming the resolvent --- reproduces the same matrix to
$5.6\times10^{-17}$ on 20 games (\texttt{implicit\_chi\_agreement}), which is the
statement that the two code paths are the same mathematics rather than merely
close.

\paragraph{Against an analytically known potential.} The population engine's
identities are exact rather than approximate: the autodiff gradient of the
Beckmann integral equals the route-cost vector to exact zero, the $DF$ symmetry
defect is exact zero, and the Fisk KKT spread is $7.1\times10^{-15}$ on real
network data. A library whose central instrument is a potentiality test should
read zero where a theorem says zero, and that is where it is checked.
