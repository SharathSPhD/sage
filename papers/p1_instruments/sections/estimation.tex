\section{Estimating $\lambda$: four routes and an honesty protocol}
\label{sec:estimation}

The rationality parameter is the most abused dial in the QRE literature ---
mis-specified demand, unmodelled heterogeneity and wrong payoff models all
try to leak into it. Our defence is redundancy (unit
\texttt{estimate.lambda}): four structurally different estimators run
together, with their disagreement treated as a diagnostic rather than a
nuisance.

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Frequency MLE} --- multinomial likelihood of observed choice
counts under $\sigma^*(\lambda)$, with a profile-likelihood confidence
interval and a \emph{flat-likelihood guard}: on any game whose QRE barely
moves with $\lambda$ (symmetric rock--paper--scissors is the extreme case,
uniform at every $\lambda$), the estimator returns a warning, never a bare
number.
\item \textbf{Autodiff MLE} --- the same objective scored by JAX
differentiation through an unrolled damped solve (convergence of the unroll
verified to $10^{-11}$ at $\lambda = 20$); agreement with the grid MLE is a
solver cross-check. Its CI is inherited from the profile likelihood and
says so explicitly.
\item \textbf{$\chi$ moment-matching} --- fit $\lambda$ to an observed
cross-response matrix; the route that works when choices are
equilibrium-degenerate but responses are measurable (the pass-through
setting of Section~\ref{sec:instruments}).
\item \textbf{Dispersion inversion} --- invert mean choice entropy, with
its own flat-entropy guard (a symmetric principal branch below its
pitchfork is unidentified and says so).
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Validation.} On well-specified synthetic data (20 (game,
$\lambda^*$) cases spanning an asymmetric anchor game and mixed-$\alpha$
family games, $\lambda^* \in [0.3, 6]$): median relative error 2.6\% (MLE),
2.8\% (dispersion), exact for $\chi$-matching from oracle responses
(artifacts \texttt{estimator\_recovery.json}).

\paragraph{The diagnostic fires when it must.} On data drawn from a
$\lambda$-\emph{mixture} (half $0.4$, half $4.0$ --- not a QRE at any single
$\lambda$) the estimator spread widens $\times 91$ over clean data and the
protocol flags misspecification instead of averaging it away
(\texttt{estimator\_misspecification.json}). Two rules follow for all data
work: report the whole family, never one number; and treat a flagged
disagreement as a finding about the model.

\paragraph{First market contact.} The same discipline held on real data
(unit \texttt{domains.electricity}): asked to rationalise the July-2026
CAISO SP15 price dispersion, the domain's stylised two-generator auction
was \emph{rejected} by its own one-moment fit --- the model's dispersion
ceiling sits 40\% below the spike-driven observation --- and the pipeline
reported the rejection rather than a $\lambda$
(\texttt{electricity\_lambda.json}). What \emph{is} claimed from that data
--- a verified pair-level detailed-balance violation in the day-ahead
series, and a genuinely at-null real-time series --- belongs to the
dissipation instruments and is developed in the companion paper
\citep{p3}.
