advance. Table~\ref{tab:calib} is this paper's argument, and everything else is

\paragraph{The $\alpha=0$ anchor: congestion on real network data.} Rosenthal
congestion games \citep{rosenthal1973} are exact potential games, and in the
population form logit route choice \emph{is} Fisk's stochastic user equilibrium
\citep{fisk1980}, whose convex potential --- the Beckmann integral plus scaled
entropy --- is known analytically. On the Braess diamond the autodiff gradient
of the Beckmann integral equals the route-cost vector exactly, which is the
project's sharpest end-to-end code check because it composes the payoff oracle,
the network, the potential and the differentiation in one identity. On real
Sioux Falls data (76 links, top-8 OD pairs, $k{=}3$ route sets from the TNTP
repository \citep{tntp}) the Fisk KKT conditions hold to $7.1\times10^{-15}$,
$DF$ is symmetric to exact zero, the toll-response reciprocity defect reads
$\R=5.6\times10^{-17}$, and the toll susceptibility matches finite differences to
$2.8\times10^{-9}$ with the sign verified against actual re-solves. The scope is
stated once and not softened: route sets are $k$-shortest restrictions and the OD
set is a subset, so computed link flows are \emph{not} comparable to the
repository's best-known equilibrium flows (a diagnostic gap of $0.735$ is
reported as a diagnostic, never a certificate); the instrument identities are
exact within the route set regardless. One practical fact is documented rather
than hidden: the damped logit-assignment map is \emph{not} a contraction on
steep-BPR networks --- it two-cycles on the Braess diamond --- which is why the
SUE path is damped Newton on the convex program.

\paragraph{The $\alpha>0$ anchor: Colonel Blotto.} Two-player Blotto over
lattice-simplex allocation grids, with budgets as an exactly observable
conjugate field. The symmetric budget-3, three-field instance reads
$\alpha=0.694$ and $\R=0.118$ at $\lambda=1.5$; an asymmetric instance reads
$\EPR=0.098$ with visible circulation; a degenerate instance (equal field values,
budget 2, every profile ties) has constant payoffs and every meter reads exactly
zero ($\EPR=5.4\times10^{-32}$) --- a correct null found while writing the
positive test and kept as a regression. One calibration surprise corrected a
documented assumption: Blotto is \emph{mixed}, not pure harmonic
($\alpha\approx0.7$, not 1). Zero-sum does not imply harmonic-pure; RPS
($\alpha=1$ exactly) remains the pure extreme, so the right-hand anchor of the
bracket sits at $\approx0.7$ for a realistic strategic system.

\paragraph{The dissipation axis is observable, and the bound is the reportable
number.} Against the exact Schnakenberg meter across a ten-level $\alpha$ sweep,
the $k$-block KLD estimator \citep{roldan2010} attains Spearman rank correlation
$1.0$ with per-level relative error of order $1\%$, and the debiased finite-time
thermodynamic-uncertainty bound \citep{barato2015,horowitz2017} lies below the
exact value at every level with median tightness $0.850$
(\texttt{estimator\_alpha\_sweep}). Two implementation facts are recorded
because they were learned the hard way and change the number: the estimator must
be debiased for $E[\bar J^2]$ and for Jensen on $1/\mathrm{Var}$, and windows
must be fixed-\emph{horizon} --- fixed-jump-count windows overshoot by about
$1.5\times$. On the named ground-truth games the estimator reads
$3\times10^{-5}$ where the exact answer is zero, and $2.228$ against an exact
$2.239$ on RPS-3 (\texttt{estimator\_ground\_truth}).
