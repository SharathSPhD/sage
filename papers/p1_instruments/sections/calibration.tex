A meter is only as credible as the systems on which its reading is known.
The $\alpha$ axis offers exactly two families with payoffs \emph{free by
construction} --- no demand estimation contaminating the reading --- and we
anchored both ends before pointing the instruments anywhere else.

\subsection{The $\alpha=0$ anchor: congestion on real network data}
Rosenthal congestion games \citep{rosenthal1973} are exact potential games; in
the population form, logit route choice \emph{is} Fisk's stochastic user
equilibrium \citep{fisk1980}, whose convex potential (Beckmann integral plus
scaled entropy) is known analytically. We implement the population engine
(payoff field $F(x)$, potentiality tested by symmetry of $DF$ --- Sandholm's
externality symmetry \citep{sandholm2010}, cited, not claimed) and solve SUE by
damped Newton on the convex program in per-OD tangent coordinates; the damped
logit-assignment map itself is \emph{not} a contraction on steep-BPR networks
(it two-cycles on the Braess diamond), a practical fact we document rather than
hide.

Calibration readings (Table~\ref{tab:calib}): on the Braess diamond, the
autodiff gradient of the Beckmann integral equals the route-cost vector to
$10^{-12}$ (an identity, and our sharpest end-to-end code check); on
\emph{real Sioux Falls data} (76 links, top-8 OD pairs, $k{=}3$ route sets from
the TNTP repository \citep{tntp}), the Fisk KKT conditions hold to
$7\times10^{-15}$, $DF$ is symmetric to machine zero, and the toll-response
reciprocity defect reads $\R=5.7\times10^{-17}$. Link tolls enter route costs
exactly linearly --- the cleanest conjugate field in the programme --- and the
toll susceptibility matches finite differences to $2.8\times10^{-9}$, with the
sign verified against actual re-solves. We state the scope once: route sets are
$k$-shortest restrictions and the OD set is a subset, so computed link flows
are \emph{not} comparable to the repository's best-known equilibrium flows (a
diagnostic gap of 0.73 is reported as a diagnostic, never a certificate); the
instrument identities are exact within the route set regardless.

\subsection{The $\alpha>0$ anchor: Colonel Blotto}
Two-player Blotto over lattice-simplex allocation grids, budgets set by the
experimenter (an exactly observable payoff perturbation). Readings: symmetric
budget-3, three equal fields --- $\alpha=0.694$, $\R=0.118$ at $\lambda=1.5$;
asymmetric field values --- $\EPR=0.098$ with visible circulation. A degenerate
instance (equal values, budget 2) has \emph{constant} payoffs --- every profile
ties --- and every meter reads exactly zero ($\EPR=5.4\times10^{-32}$): a
correct null found while writing the positive test, kept as a regression. One
calibration surprise worth naming: Blotto is \emph{mixed}, not pure harmonic
($\alpha\approx0.7$, not 1) --- zero-sum does not imply harmonic-pure; RPS
($\alpha=1$ exactly) remains the pure extreme.
