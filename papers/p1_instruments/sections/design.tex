\paragraph{Two engines, and a rule about what may cross between them.}
\strataq{} is a uv-workspace monorepo in JAX. Engine~1 is the finite strategic
form (dense payoff tensors, normalisation, the Hodge decomposition, the response
instruments, the solvers, the exact dynamics); Engine~2 is population games
(routing networks, Fisk stochastic user equilibrium \citep{fisk1980},
population-level susceptibility). A third engine for Bayesian games with type
spaces is \emph{deliberately not started}, by a recorded decision, because the
finite-strategic-form machinery does not apply to it as written.

Domains --- congestion, Blotto, pricing, electricity --- are plugins declaring
exactly five objects (payoff oracle, action-grid builder, conjugate-field
specification, dataset loader, learn page) and making \emph{zero} core changes,
enforced by a commit hook and in CI: a domain may import its own declared engine
and nothing else, and a commit touching both a domain and the core must cite a
recorded architecture decision by id. The invariant is that \emph{a domain that
needs core changes is not a domain, it is an engine}.

\paragraph{Refusal is a return value.} The conjugate-field declaration names the
observable payoff perturbation the response instruments differentiate with
respect to --- link tolls, budgets, wholesale cost. A domain that cannot name one
declares \texttt{NONE}, and the response instruments then \emph{refuse to run}
rather than returning a number with no operational meaning. The pattern recurs:
\texttt{fit()} reports a flat likelihood as the interval it truly is (the whole
search window) and withholds the point estimate; \texttt{diagnose()} reports an
unidentifiable coordinate as a bound, or as a named pair of quadrants it cannot
separate, never as \texttt{None}.

\paragraph{Float64 and the tangent space are correctness requirements, not
preferences.} JAX x64 is enabled at import: $(I-SB)$ is near-singular exactly
where the interesting behaviour is, and float32 is not acceptable there. All
linear algebra involving $(I-SB)$ runs on
$T=\bigoplus_i\{v:\mathbf{1}^\top v=0\}$ through an explicit Helmert basis,
because $C_i=\operatorname{diag}(\sigma_i)-\sigma_i\sigma_i^\top$ is
rank-deficient by construction and skipping the projection manufactures a
spurious zero eigenvalue --- a false criticality reading. Decomposition always
acts on the \emph{normalised} game, since full externality symmetry is
sufficient but not necessary for potentiality of the raw game
\citep{legacci2024}.

\paragraph{No literal constants in library code.} Tolerances, seeds, grids and
$\lambda$ ranges come from a typed configuration schema, on a named ladder:
$10^{-12}$ for exact identities, $10^{-10}$ for decomposition and solver
convergence, $10^{-8}$ for oracle cross-checks against an external
implementation, $10^{-6}$ for finite differences. Every stochastic call site
takes an explicit PRNG key folded from a root recorded in the resolved
configuration; there is no global RNG. Every reading reports both $\lambda$ and
the normalised precision $\bar\lambda=\lambda\cdot(\text{payoff range})$, because
payoffs are rescaled internally and the scale folds into $\lambda$ --- a confound
that has caught one of this project's own experiments and is now reported by
construction.

\paragraph{Two surfaces, one machinery.} The upper surface is
\texttt{strataq.problems} (state a problem, get a solution object); the lower is
the instrument layer (\texttt{logit\_qre}, \texttt{chi\_equilibrium},
\texttt{reciprocity\_defect}, \texttt{alpha}, \texttt{diagnose}). The upper calls
the lower and never reimplements it, and the facade's numbers are gated against
the research artifacts they claim to reproduce.
