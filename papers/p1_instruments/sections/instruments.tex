Notation: players $i\in\{1,\dots,N\}$ with finite action sets, mixed profiles
$\sigma_i$, logit precision $\lambda_i$; $C_i=\operatorname{diag}(\sigma_i)-\sigma_i\sigma_i^\top$;
$S=\operatorname{blockdiag}(\lambda_iC_i)$; $B_{ij}(a,b)=\partial U_i(a)/\partial\sigma_j(b)$
the cross-payoff operator. All linear algebra involving $(I-SB)$ is performed
on the tangent space $T=\bigoplus_i\{v:\mathbf{1}^\top v=0\}$ via an explicit
Helmert basis; $C_i$ is rank-deficient by construction, and skipping this
projection manufactures a spurious zero eigenvalue --- a false criticality
reading. Decomposition always acts on the \emph{normalised} (effective) game:
full externality symmetry is sufficient but not necessary for potentiality of
the raw game \citep{legacci2024}.

\subsection{The susceptibility meter (exact + derived)}
The opponents-frozen response to an own-payoff field $h_i$ is the static
fluctuation--dissipation identity $\chipart=\lambda_i C_i$ (exact; verified to
$10^{-12}$ by autodiff of the log-partition). With strategic feedback,
total differentiation of the fixed point gives the \emph{strategic resolvent}
\begin{equation}
\chieq \;=\; \frac{d\sigma^*}{dh} \;=\; (I-SB)^{-1}S \qquad\text{on } T,
\label{eq:resolvent}
\end{equation}
likely folklore as comparative statics (we cite defensively) but load-bearing
here: the same operator is the contraction certificate ($\rho(SB)<1\Rightarrow$
unique QRE), the bifurcation detector (eigenvalues of $SB$ crossing~1), and the
implicit-differentiation kernel (our custom VJP solves the transposed tangent
system; its Jacobian reproduces \eqref{eq:resolvent} to machine epsilon).
Verification: $\chieq$ matches central finite differences of the re-solved QRE
to $1.3\times10^{-8}$ over 50 random games.

\subsection{The reciprocity meter (derived)}
\begin{result}[Reciprocity transfer]\label{res:transfer}
$\chieq$ is symmetric $\iff S(B-B^\top)S=0 \iff$ (full support) $B=B^\top$ on
$T$ $\iff$ the normalised game has zero harmonic component.
\end{result}
The proof is three lines of resolvent algebra; the \emph{content} is that
strategic feedback neither creates nor destroys reciprocity, so the observable
equilibrium response inherits the symmetry of the unobservable payoff operator
exactly. A prior-art sweep (recorded in the repository, including the search
scope) found no prior statement. Define the reciprocity defect
$\R = \|\chieq-\chieq{}^\top\|_F\,/\,\|\chieq+\chieq{}^\top\|_F \in [0,\infty)$.
Because Result~\ref{res:transfer} is a symmetry statement, \emph{whether}
$\R=0$ is $\lambda$-free --- nothing for a noise parameter to absorb, which is
our lead response to the \citet{hhk2008} non-falsifiability critique. Two
properties discovered in calibration and now documented on the instrument:
$\R$ is a norm ratio, unbounded above (matching pennies reads 1.2); and its
\emph{magnitude} scales with $\lambda$ (approximately $\propto\lambda$ at small
$\lambda$), so only the zero test is $\lambda$-robust.

\subsection{The harmonic-fraction meter (exact)}
The game graph is a Cartesian product of complete graphs; its Laplacian is a
Kronecker sum with closed-form eigenspaces indexed by which coordinates vary.
The Candogan projections therefore reduce to separable per-axis
centering/averaging --- an FFT-like transform, $O(\text{tensor size}\times N)$,
no iterative solver --- with the potential/harmonic split of each subset sector
given by the degree-weighted mean across the players in the sector. We verify
orthogonality (in the response-graph inner product), idempotence, exact
reconstruction, and invariance of $\alpha$ under strategic equivalence, each to
$10^{-9}$ or better. Families at exact target $\alpha$ are generated by mixing
unit-norm potential and harmonic components --- never by perturbing a Jacobian,
whose skew part is coordinate-dependent \citep{legacci2024}.

\subsection{The dissipation meter (exact)}
Single-site logit revision on the joint profile space is a Glauber chain whose
stationary distribution, probability currents $J^*$, and entropy production
rate $\EPR$ we compute exactly (dense path, small games). In an exact potential
game the revision probabilities are heat-bath conditionals of
$\pi\propto e^{\lambda\Phi}$: detailed balance holds transition-by-transition
(verified to $1.5\times10^{-16}$), $J^*=0$, $\EPR=0$. Off potentiality the
chain holds a non-equilibrium steady state that circulates and dissipates.

\subsection{Solvers and the phase locator}
Damped iteration, magnetic mirror descent \citep{sokota2023} (last-iterate;
agrees with damped to $7\times10^{-13}$), and pseudo-arclength continuation of
the principal branch (agrees with Gambit's homotopy \citep{turocy2005} to
$10^{-8}$, and passes through the coordination pitchfork without stalling).
The spectrum of $SB$ on $T$ supplies $\rho$, the distance to criticality
$1-\rho$, and bifurcation typing: $B=B^\top$ forces a real spectrum, so
sustained cycles under logit dynamics cannot arise in full potential games ---
verified: the spectrum is exactly real on every potential game tested across
$\lambda\in[0.5,10]$, and visibly complex on harmonic games (cf.\ the logit
limit cycles of \citealt{hommes2012} at $\alpha=1$).
