\textbf{What is known, and cited as such.} The entropy-regularised
characterisation of logit response, the CGF structure of the log-partition,
Gibbs stationarity of logit dynamics in potential games, and the
symmetry-characterisation of potentiality are textbook
\citep{mckelvey1995,blume1993,monderer1996,sandholm2010,balduzzi2018,matejka2015}.
The intrinsic potential/harmonic decomposition is \citet{candogan2011}. An
earlier draft of the research programme claimed the symmetry characterisation
as new; the project's own sanity check caught and downgraded it --- that
correction is part of the claim ledger's public history, and we mention it as
evidence the tier system binds us, not only others.

\textbf{Nearest live work.} \citet{legacci2024} prove, in Shahshahani geometry,
that harmonic games are incompressible under continuous-time exponential
weights --- volume preservation, constants of motion, Poincar\'e recurrence ---
and leave discrete-time behaviour and mixed games open. Our programme sits at
a different layer: discrete logit QRE, the observable equilibrium-response
matrix, and non-equilibrium observables (currents, dissipation), with
calibration on real data. Their precision fix (potentiality of the
\emph{effective} game; Lemma~C.2) is baked into our decomposition path. We
found no prior statement of the reciprocity transfer (Result~1) or of
response-matrix asymmetry as an observable proxy for harmonic content; the
sweep and its scope are recorded in the repository's literature file.

\textbf{What this paper does not claim.} $\R$ is not a universal circulation
gauge (Finding~3 refutes exactly that); $\lambda$ estimated from data absorbs
unmodelled heterogeneity, and low $\lambda$ is not proof of irrationality
\citep{hhk2008}; the Sioux Falls exercise is calibration on computed
equilibria, not an empirical claim about drivers; and the thermodynamic
language is exact only where the identities are exact --- for non-potential
games what survives is the NESS framing, which is where the interesting
readings live. Empirical contact (scanner panels, electricity offer curves,
$\lambda$ estimator families and conduct testing) is the subject of the
programme's later papers; the closest empirical precedent for the dissipation
angle is \citet{xu2011}, who identify an Edgeworth cycle via nonzero entropy
production in experimental market data.
