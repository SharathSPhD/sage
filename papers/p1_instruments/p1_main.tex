\begin{abstract}
We present \texttt{strataq}, an open JAX library that treats logit quantal-response
equilibrium (QRE) analysis as \emph{instrumentation}: a susceptibility meter, a
reciprocity meter, an entropy-production meter, and a phase locator, each an
executable function with a stated confidence tier and a regenerable calibration
artifact. The exact backbone is known (QRE = entropy-regularised response; Gibbs
stationarity in potential games); the library's contribution is (i) the
\emph{reciprocity transfer}: the observable equilibrium response
$\chieq=(I-SB)^{-1}S$ is symmetric \emph{iff} the normalised game has zero
harmonic component, so strategic feedback neither creates nor destroys
reciprocity---making the reciprocity defect $\R$ a payoff-free, $\lambda$-free
\emph{test} of potentiality; (ii) a separable Kronecker implementation of the
Candogan decomposition giving the harmonic fraction $\alpha$ in near-linear time;
(iii) exact-tier dynamics meters (currents, entropy production) on the joint-profile
Glauber chain; and (iv) calibration of every meter at both ends of the $\alpha$
axis with payoffs known by construction: Rosenthal congestion on real Sioux Falls
network data ($\R=5.7\times10^{-17}$, Fisk KKT $7\times10^{-15}$) and Colonel
Blotto ($\alpha=0.69$, $\R=0.12$). Pointing the calibrated instruments at seeded
game families produced three findings we did not predict: $\R$ magnitudes scale
with $\lambda$ (only the zero test is $\lambda$-free); potential games
\emph{escape} criticality at high $\lambda$ through equilibrium concentration,
opening a supercritical wedge only for $\alpha\gtrsim0.5$; and the response and
dissipation layers decouple structurally in the near-harmonic regime---the
correlation between response asymmetry and entropy production reverses sign at
$\alpha=0.95$, and no renormalisation of the response matrix repairs it. Every
quantitative statement in this paper regenerates from fixed seeds
(\texttt{make reproduce}); Appendix~A maps each number to its artifact.
\end{abstract}
