\section{Setup: the joint-profile chain and its four meters}
\label{sec:setup}

A finite game with players $i=1..N$ and payoff tensors $u_i$ defines
\emph{Glauber--logit dynamics}: revision opportunities arrive at unit rate per
player, and the revising player redraws an action from
$\mathrm{softmax}(\lambda\, u_i(\cdot, a_{-i}))$. This is a continuous-time
Markov jump process on the joint profile space $\prod_i A_i$. Two exact facts
anchor everything below (both are permanent regression tests in the library,
verified to $10^{-10}$--$10^{-16}$; tiers per the claims ledger).

\paragraph{Potential games are thermal equilibrium.} If the game admits an
exact potential $\Phi$, the dynamics are reversible with Gibbs stationary law
$\pi \propto e^{\lambda\Phi}$ \citep{blume1993}: detailed balance holds, the
stationary probability current $J^*(a,a') = \pi(a)w(a{\to}a') -
\pi(a')w(a'{\to}a)$ vanishes edge-by-edge, and the Schnakenberg entropy
production rate \citep{schnakenberg1976}
\begin{equation}
\EPR \;=\; \tfrac12 \sum_{a,a'} \big[\pi(a)w(a{\to}a') - \pi(a')w(a'{\to}a)\big]
\log\frac{\pi(a)w(a{\to}a')}{\pi(a')w(a'{\to}a)} \;\ge 0
\end{equation}
is exactly zero.

\paragraph{Harmonic content is the drive.} The Hodge decomposition
\citep{candogan2011} splits any game into potential, harmonic and
non-strategic components; we write $\alpha \in [0,1]$ for the harmonic
fraction of the normalised game. At $\alpha>0$ the same dynamics settle into a
non-equilibrium steady state: stationary but circulating ($J^* \neq 0$),
dissipating at rate $\EPR > 0$.

\paragraph{The meters.} At each $(\alpha, \lambda)$ we read:
(1) the \emph{feedback gain} $\rho(SB)$ --- spectral radius of the strategic
feedback operator in $\chieq = (I-SB)^{-1}S$, with $\rho \ge 1$ marking a
near-singular resolvent (criticality);
(2) the \emph{reciprocity defect}
$\R = \lVert \chieq - \chieq{}^{\top}\rVert_F / \lVert \chieq + \chieq{}^{\top}\rVert_F$,
which is zero iff the game is potential, at every $\lambda$ \citep{p1};
(3) the \emph{exact} $\EPR$ of the joint-profile chain; and
(4) trajectory estimators of $\EPR$ that see only an observed path
(Section~\ref{sec:estimators}).
Families with $\alpha$ prescribed exactly are generated by unit-normalising
the potential component of one random game and the harmonic component of
another and mixing $(1-\alpha)\hat P + \alpha \hat H$; all sweeps below use
100 games per $\alpha$ level at seed 20260808 (regeneration:
\texttt{make reproduce}).

\paragraph{Positioning.} The behaviour of learning dynamics between the
potential and harmonic extremes is an acknowledged obstacle
\citep{legacci2024}. Our angle is complementary and, to our knowledge,
unoccupied: treat the stationary state \emph{thermodynamically} --- currents,
dissipation, response symmetry, uncertainty bounds --- with instruments
calibrated where the correct reading is provable.
