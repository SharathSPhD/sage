\begin{abstract}
Logit revision dynamics on a finite game define a Markov jump process on the joint
profile space whose stationary state is a genuine thermodynamic object: a Gibbs
equilibrium exactly when the game is potential, and a dissipative non-equilibrium
steady state otherwise. We sweep the two-parameter plane spanned by the harmonic
fraction $\alpha$ of the game's Hodge decomposition and the logit precision
$\lambda$, reading four calibrated meters at every point --- spectral criticality
$\rho(SB)$, reciprocity defect $\R$, exact entropy production $\EPR$, and its
trajectory estimators --- and report three facts we did not predict.
(i)~\emph{Criticality escape}: on potential games $\rho(SB)$ is non-monotone in
$\lambda$ --- equilibrium concentration kills the choice covariance faster than
$\lambda$ amplifies it --- while harmonic games cross $\rho=1$ and stay there,
opening a \emph{supercritical wedge} whose frontier descends in $\alpha$
(median onset $\alpha=0.5$ on the coarse $9\times11$ map; a bisected
frontier on a finer sweep finds a crossing only for $\alpha\ge0.55$, with
$\lambda_c$ descending $7.8\to3.0$ over $\alpha\in[0.55,0.80]$).
(ii)~\emph{Decoupling}: dissipation and response asymmetry co-move almost
perfectly along $\alpha$ ($\rho_S=0.993$ marginally) yet, conditional on
$\alpha$, their correlation \emph{collapses} in the near-harmonic regime, and
at $m=3$, $\lambda=1.2$, $N=2$ it crosses into negative territory
($-0.355$). The collapse is the robust statement and it reproduces at every
precision and action count tested; the negative \emph{sign} is scoped, moving
with $\lambda$ and --- at fixed payoff scale --- with $m$, and it is quoted
only with its $(m,\lambda,N)$ attached. A mechanism experiment shows the
collapse is structural: the equilibrium susceptibility is a local derivative,
the entropy production a global flux functional, and no renormalisation of the
former recovers the latter's ordering. A repair hypothesis we proposed is
refuted by its own preregistered test. \textbf{Everything in this arc is
$N=2$}: at three and four players the meters do not couple at low $\alpha$
either, so the stratified design has no baseline to collapse from and the
result does not transfer.
(iii)~\emph{Estimability, through to real data}: a $k$-th order Markov KLD
estimator tracks the exact meter with rank correlation $1.0$; a debiased
finite-time thermodynamic-uncertainty bound certifies dissipation from two
cumulants of one current; and, pointed at a real power market (CAISO SP15,
July 2026), the instruments deliver a verified detection --- day-ahead hourly
prices violate pair-level detailed balance at $\approx 1.1$ nats/day,
concentrated in scarcity weeks --- alongside an equally instructive at-null
verdict for the 5-minute series. That verdict is at-null \emph{in the matched
(reversibilized-Markov) null class}, and deliberately not called certified: an
intermediate ``certified null'' claim was \textbf{retracted} on adversarial
review when the spectral surrogate classes were shown not to bracket the data's
persistence, and what survives is a no-detection at $k=1$ and $k=2$ against a
null that does match persistence.
Every number regenerates from fixed seeds; every claim carries its confidence
tier and its falsifier.
\end{abstract}
