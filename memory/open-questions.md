13. **How would a quadrant-III system ever be observed?** For an exact two-player normal-form game ℛ and EPR share a zero, so ℛ > 0 ⟹ EPR > 0 and quadrant III (asymmetric response, no circulation) is **unreachable within one such game** — it is reachable only when the two coordinates come from two different instruments on two different kinds of data. Open: what design distinguishes a genuine quadrant-III system from (a) an observation-window artefact (the RTM 5-min case, where dwell dominates and k=1 sees nothing) and (b) a coarse-graining artefact? Any candidate reading needs its dissipation coordinate at more than one resolution against a persistence-matched null.
